Timing Is Everything

Done Right, Done Once

Installing smart home technology during construction isn't just easier, it's the only way to do it properly.

During Construction
  • Hidden wiring, clean infrastructure
  • Optimal speaker & device placement
  • Seamless integration with your trades
  • No patching, no drywall damage
vs
After Construction
  • Exposed wires, visible conduit
  • Limited placement options
  • Disruptive retrofitting
  • 2–3× higher installation cost

Prewiring during the build phase is the smart choice for your buyers, your brand, and everyone's bottom line.
