Our Story

Imagine a Home That Works As Hard As You Do

Imagine having a better experience in your home or office every single day — using technology that's seamlessly woven into your life and décor, yet effortless to understand and use. Systems that simply work. Mundane routines automated so you never have to think about them again.

Picture a haven of pure joy and relaxation where you can connect or disconnect, entertain family, friends, or coworkers, and feel completely safe — even when you're miles away. These are the experiences we design and build every day at Listen Hear.

We're passionate about home and commercial technology: audio, video, home theater, smart home automation, lighting, motorized shades, and more. That "more" represents the tireless curiosity that drives us — always exploring what's new, always searching for the right solution. Our family business was founded on over 30 years of this passion, and it remains at the core of everything we do.

Our Founders

A Dream 30 Years in the Making

Listen Hear was founded — and continues to be led — by husband and wife team Albert and Leslie Wurst, both North Hills, Pittsburgh natives. Albert's love of technology started in his teens, researching and installing a car stereo system in his Z28 Camaro. His passion quickly caught the attention of a local shop owner, who hired him on the spot.

Barely out of high school, Albert joined the IT department at Federated Investors, launching a successful 33-year career navigating complex systems — all while never stopping his tinkering and learning. In 2008, an opportunity presented itself and a dream came true: Albert and Leslie launched Listen Hear, channeling every year of passion and experience into a business built to serve their community.

Today, Albert remains active in designing tailored solutions for every client, while Leslie leads the administrative and marketing functions that keep the team running smoothly. Their commitment and enthusiasm for the people of Pittsburgh remain as strong as ever.

How We Work

Great Relationships Foster Great Work

Whether it's new construction, an existing home, or a commercial space — indoors or out — our process is built around you.

01

We Listen First

Every project starts with a conversation. We take the time to truly understand your vision, lifestyle, and goals — before we ever recommend a single product or draw a single wire.

02

We Collaborate

We work closely with architects, builders, decorators, electricians, and contractors — integrating seamlessly into the broader project team to ensure technology enhances the design rather than fighting it.

03

We Design & Install

Our team of highly trained, certified technicians handles every installation with precision and pride. We make sure everything is right — and that you fully understand your new systems — before we pack up.

04

We're Here for Life

The relationship doesn't end at installation. We're available 24/7 for the lifetime of your system — for support, upgrades, or simply a question about your setup. That's the Listen Hear promise.
